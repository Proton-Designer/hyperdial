# Intern quick start

You're calling leads for a HyperDial campaign. Here's everything you need.

## Signing in

Your manager sent you an invite link. Open it, set a password, and you're
in — there's no separate "create account" page; the invite link is the only
way to get an account.

## Before you start dialing

**Allow microphone access** when your browser asks. If you miss the prompt or
say no, the app can't put you on the call — you'll see an error instead of
silently failing.

**Use one browser tab.** HyperDial only lets your phone line be active in one
tab at a time. If you open a second tab (or a second window) while you're
already set up in the first, that second one will show:

> **HyperDial is open in another tab**

That's not a bug — close the extra tab and keep using the first one. If you
see that message and you're SURE no other tab is open, close the browser
entirely and reopen it.

## Starting a session

1. Open the **Dialer** tab.
2. You'll see the campaigns your manager assigned you to, with how many leads
   are queued in each.
3. Pick one and click **Start**.
4. Your line connects first (you'll see "Connecting your line…" for a couple
   seconds) — then dialing starts on its own. You don't need to dial anything
   yourself.

If you see "No campaigns assigned yet — ask your manager," that's exactly
what it sounds like: nobody's put you on a campaign yet.

## What the banner means

The big banner at the top of the Dialer always tells you exactly what's
happening:

| Banner                             | What it means                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ready — waiting to dial**        | Your line is connected. Dialing is about to start.                                                                                              |
| **Ringing N lines**                | Several contacts are being dialed at once. Nothing for you to do yet.                                                                           |
| **Connecting…**                    | Someone just answered — you're about to be bridged in.                                                                                          |
| **Connected — \<name\> \<timer\>** | You're live with that person. Talk.                                                                                                             |
| **Wrapping up**                    | The call ended. You have to submit a disposition before dialing continues — see below.                                                          |
| **Paused**                         | You clicked Pause. Nothing dials until you hit Resume.                                                                                          |
| **Agent audio lost — Reconnect**   | _Your_ line dropped, not the customer's — they're still connected, waiting. Click Reconnect (or wait a moment; it often reconnects on its own). |

## The wrap-up form

After every call, a form pops up and **you can't dismiss it** — it's the only
way back to dialing (Stop still works if you need to end the whole session).

- Press a **number key (1–9)** to pick the disposition that matches the call,
  or click it.
- Add a note if useful.
- Press **Enter** (or click Submit).

Dialing resumes automatically once you submit.

## Pause, Resume, Stop

- **Pause** — stops new lines from dialing. Use it if you need a minute.
  Existing calls in progress aren't affected.
- **Resume** — undoes Pause.
- **Stop** — ends the whole session. Do this at the end of your shift, or if
  you need to switch to a different campaign.

## If your line drops mid-call

You'll see **"Agent audio lost — Reconnect."** The customer is **not**
disconnected — they're still on the line, just not able to hear you for a
moment. HyperDial tries to reconnect you automatically once; if that doesn't
work, click **Reconnect** yourself.

## Something's wrong

- **"HyperDial is open in another tab"** — see above, close the extra tab.
- **A red error banner about your line** — usually a mic-permission or
  network issue. Reload the page and try again.
- **Anything else** — tell your manager. Include roughly when it happened and
  what the banner said.
